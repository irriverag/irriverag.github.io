← Scheduled Backups

Coursework · Fall 2026 · Bash

Another Bash script from the same lab. It waits 5 minutes after startup to let the system settle, then makes a backup every minute, with a timestamp on each one.

Problems I ran into

The timestamps were wrong. They were supposed to show the seconds, but instead they showed the number of seconds since the Unix epoch. The fix was just capitalizing the S in the script. It was a tiny change, but it made me pay a lot more attention to detail.

The service looked dead. When I ran systemctl status on it, it showed up as inactive (dead) instead of active (exited). I fixed it by making the script always show up as active.

What I learned: scheduling tasks that repeat, writing scripts that keep working without me babysitting them, and that one wrong letter can change everything.
